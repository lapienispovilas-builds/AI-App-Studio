# Safety Check-In

## Idea

A safety app that automatically alerts trusted contacts if you don't check in after a date, meeting or journey.

---

## Problem

- Who is this for?

Women who regularly go on dates, meet strangers or travel alone.

- What painful problem do they have?

Friends and family worry about their safety. Women often remember to text "I'm home" but sometimes forget.

- How do they solve it today?

Manual texts

Location sharing

Phone calls

Nothing

- Why are current solutions not good enough?

They rely on remembering to send a message.

---
