# ADHD Spending Log

## Idea

Impulsive purchasing app that helps to track impulsive purchases and create awareness about them

---

## Problem

- Who is this for?

Adults with ADHD who struggle with impulsive purchases.

- What painful problem do they have?

They often buy things impulsively and regret it later.

- How do they solve it today?

Budgeting apps, notes, spreadsheets, memory.

- Why are current solutions not good enough?

They focus on budgeting after the money is spent instead of creating awareness before or immediately after impulse purchases.

---

## Target User

- Primary audience:

Adults with ADHD

- Age:

16-40

- Occupation:

Any

- Pain frequency:

Daily

- Where do they spend time online?

Reddit
TikTok
Instagram
ADHD communities

---

## Core Hypothesis

We believe people with ADHD will interrupt an impulse purchase (or immediately after it) to log it if it takes less than 10 seconds.
