# Subscription Tracker

## Idea

A simple subscription tracker that helps people stop wasting money on forgotten recurring payments.

---

## Problem

- Who is this for?

Anyone paying for multiple subscriptions.

- What painful problem do they have?

They forget recurring payments until money leaves their account.

- How do they solve it today?

Memory.

Bank statements.

Nothing.

- Why are current solutions not good enough?

Too complicated.

Require bank integrations.

---

## Target User

- Primary audience:

Adults

- Age:

18-50

- Occupation:

Any

- Pain frequency:

Monthly / weekly

- Where do they spend time online?

TikTok
Instagram
Facebook

---

## Core Hypothesis

We believe people want a simple overview without connecting their bank.
